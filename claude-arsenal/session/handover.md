## Four things worth carrying forward

**A description is a claim, and claims get checked.** Writing "Unique within the
document" on `group.id` was the most valuable line of #39, because it was false:
three groups sharing two ids validated, rendered, and drew one caption of three.

**Measure the thing, not a proxy for it.** The first crossing count parsed paths
out of the finished SVG and reported 31 across 14 drawings — mostly timeline axes
and chart lines, which are not routes. Counting the router's own `Route` objects
gave 14 across 8. Every number in #40 comes from the second method.

**Delete the change you cannot demonstrate.** A second edit to `_targets` in #40
fixed a real inversion, changed exactly one drawing, and removed no crossing. It
was reverted. The suite carries what is proven; the docstring carries the rest.

**Wait for Qodo before merging.** The owner asked for this explicitly. Qodo found
a real trap in #40's test helper — `fit` retries on `FitError`, so accumulating
routes across calls could measure a layout nobody sees.

- **A — measured, not started.** `geometry.normalise` grows every box in a group
  to the largest width *and* height among them. Across the 86 redraws: 445 boxes
  through it, **13% taller than their own text** (worst 3.0×, in `tic__rgpd`),
  54% wider, and 19% of all box area exists only because a peer needed it. The
  widening is the alignment that makes peers read as peers; the shared **height**
  is the harm, and is what "size each box for its own text, subject to a common
  alignment" asks to end. Callers: `grid.py` ×3, `graph.py`, `cycle.py`. Expect
  most of the 26 reference drawings to move — `make gallery` is the evidence.
