## Goal

drawspec should be usable by anyone who needs an explanatory diagram, not just
engineers. Find the diagram kinds people actually need that drawspec still can't draw,
and turn the worthwhile ones into proposals. Implementing them is follow-up work.

## Method (our way, per `docs/kinds-wanted.md`)

1. Survey broadly beyond software, for example: education (course notes, science,
   maths), business (strategy, org, finance), operations and runbooks, product/UX,
   law and policy, health, data and research papers.
2. Collect real evidence for each candidate kind: at least two public, real diagrams
   it would replace. Record where they came from, but never copy their content.
3. Classify each candidate against the 26 current kinds: covered (which kind) / small
   feature on an existing kind / new kind / out of scope. "Out of scope" follows the
   README's "What it is not" section.
4. Write `docs/research/kinds-survey.md` with the table, the evidence links, a ranking
   by value and size, and the open questions for the owner.
5. For the top candidates, write proposals in `docs/proposals/<kind>.md` using the
   existing proposal format, with our own examples and JSON, and list them in
   `docs/proposals/README.md`.

## Acceptance gate

```bash
test -s docs/research/kinds-survey.md
grep -q '| covered' docs/research/kinds-survey.md || grep -qi 'covered' docs/research/kinds-survey.md
[ "$(grep -c 'http' docs/research/kinds-survey.md)" -ge 10 ]
```
