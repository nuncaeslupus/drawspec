Design: `docs/proposals/text.md` (phase 2). Text output for `flow`, `states`, `lanes`:
the layered engine's rank and order on a character grid, edges routed orthogonally
through free cells, `┼` only at real crossings, plus a text budget (nodes, crossings)
that refuses what would print as noise.

**Width is the open risk** (owner: "try with 80, flows may need more"). Before settling
the default, measure: render every `flow`/`states`/`lanes` reference document and gallery
fixture at 80, 100 and 120 columns, both directions (`TB` usually fits narrower than
`LR`), and record how many fit at each in the PR. If 80 refuses a meaningful share,
propose a per-kind default (e.g. 100 for graph kinds) to the owner rather than choosing
silently. `--width` always overrides.

Tests first, in `tests/test_text_graph.py`: the phase-1 invariants (width, labels,
charset, trailing whitespace) for the graph kinds; every edge's endpoints touch their
boxes; crossings drawn equal crossings counted; the budget refusal points at the
document; a width-fit report test that prints the 80/100/120 table.
