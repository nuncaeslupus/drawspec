Owner answers to `docs/proposals/neuralnet.md`'s questions:

* `neurons` draws an arrow into each drawn input neuron and out of each output neuron
  (Nielsen's figures).
* `blocks` gains branches: a layer entry `{"branches": [{"layers": [...]}, ...]}` draws
  two to four parallel towers (one to three layers each) between the layer before it and
  the layer after it, which joins them — GoogLeNet's Inception module. Other branching
  stays refused (first/last position, two branch sets in a row, nesting, skips or
  repeats naming a tower's layer) with the reason.
