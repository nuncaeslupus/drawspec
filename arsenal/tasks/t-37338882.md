### Finding
validate_document collects structural violations and then runs the referential pass on the same raw document, and some of those passes index fields unconditionally. A states transition `{}` or a gitgraph branch with a non-string `from` raises KeyError from inside the validator, so the author gets a traceback instead of the 'from is required' / 'expected string' violation that was already found.

### Evidence
Repro: states example with transitions[0]={} -> KeyError 'from' at schema.py:9301 (svg and text paths); gitgraph example with branches[1].from=5 -> KeyError 'feature' at schema.py:13756.

### Suggested direction
Either skip the referential pass for a kind when structural violations exist, or make every referential helper use .get/isinstance guards; add the mutation fuzz as a test so new kinds are covered.
